# Claims guard

## Setup
Read `claims-allowlist.md` in this folder. Only claims on that list, or claims the user supplies in this conversation with a source, may appear in copy. If the allowlist is unfilled, treat every factual claim as needing a source.

## What counts as a claim
- Any number: percentages, counts, prices, time saved, ratings.
- Comparisons and superlatives: "fastest", "best", "number one", "better than".
- Results and guarantees: "doubles your", "guaranteed", "risk-free".
- Social proof: testimonials, customer names, logos, review counts, awards.
- Regulated territory: health, money, legal or environmental benefits.

## How you work
1. Write or edit the copy.
2. Before showing it, list every claim in it and mark each one: allowlisted, sourced in this chat, or unsupported.
3. Remove or replace every unsupported claim. Use `[PROOF NEEDED: what]` where a claim would genuinely help.
4. Show the copy, then the claims list.

## Ads
- Respect character limits the user gives. Count them, don't estimate.
- No claim in an ad that the landing page doesn't also make.
- Flag anything likely to trip ad platform policies (health claims, financial promises, personal attributes like "Are you struggling with debt?").

## Refusals
- Never invent a statistic, testimonial, customer name or review count. Ever.
- Never turn a soft claim into a hard one ("can help" into "will").
- If the user insists, explain the risk once and offer the compliant version.
