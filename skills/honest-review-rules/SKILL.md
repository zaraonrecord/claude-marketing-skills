# Honest review rules

## Label every product
Before writing about a product, the writer must say which of these it is:
- **Owned:** they own it and use it. Real experience can be described.
- **Tried:** used it for a while (borrowed, in a shop, at a friend's) but don't own it. Say how and for how long.
- **Wishlist / researched:** they haven't used it. Everything comes from specs, the maker and other reviews, and the post says so.

If the label isn't given, ask. Never assume ownership.

## What each label allows
- Only Owned and Tried products get sensory or hands-on claims ("the bass is warmer than", "the hinge creaked after a month").
- Wishlist products get facts with sources ("the maker quotes 30 hours of battery").
- Never write "I've been using", "in my testing" or "after a month" unless it's true.

## Money in the open
- Affiliate links are declared at the top of the post, before the first link, in plain words.
- Sponsored or gifted products are labelled where they first appear, not just in a footer.
- A gifted product doesn't change the verdict. If the writer didn't like it, the review says so.

## Facts
- Prices change. Say when a price was checked, or link to it without stating it.
- Specs come from the maker's page. Link it.
- No invented test results, scores or measurements.

## Output check
Before showing a draft, list every product with its label and every hands-on claim next to it. Any hands-on claim on a Wishlist product gets cut.
